About Us

Adventure Escape SA

We help people discover outdoor adventures and explore the natural beauty of South Africa.

Our Story

Adventure Escape SA is designed to make discovering adventure activities easier. Visitors can browse experiences, learn about activities and estimate costs before planning an adventure.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help individuals, families, tourists, schools and organisations find outdoor experiences that suit their interests and plans.
